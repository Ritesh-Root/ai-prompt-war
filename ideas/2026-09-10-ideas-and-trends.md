## 2. Today's ideas (pick one, try it today)

### Idea A — WhatsApp AI receptionist for your shop/clinic (15 min setup thinking)
- **Problem:** missed calls = missed orders. Customers already have WhatsApp; they do not want a new app.
- **Offer:** auto-reply with catalogue + hours, take bookings/orders, send reminders, track "where is my order?".
- **First prompt to try (in PromptShala Playground):**
  > "Write a polite Hindi WhatsApp auto-reply for my kirana store: shop hours 9am–9pm, Diwali offer 10% off on dry fruits, ask the customer for name + item list + delivery address, in 5 short points."
- **Why now:** festival footfall is building; every missed chat is lost margin while oil/delivery costs rise.

### Idea B — Festival reels-to-DM kit (no website needed)
- **Problem:** small sellers think they need a website first. The winners built community first.
- **Offer:** 9 reels (3 product, 3 customer, 3 behind-the-scenes) + DM script + price list image. Post daily for 9 days.
- **First prompt to try:**
  > "Give me 9 Instagram reel ideas for my boutique's wedding-lehenga collection, with Hindi captions and a WhatsApp DM reply script, in simple language, as a table."
- **Why now:** D2C discovery is reels-first; Diwali wedding season prep starts now.

### Idea C — GST-friendly smart billing nudge
- **Problem:** late payments + e-invoice mistakes eat profit.
- **Offer:** one template that auto-calculates GST, generates the e-invoice line, and sends a polite Day-3/Day-7 payment reminder on WhatsApp.
- **First prompt to try:**
  > "Draft a formal but polite payment reminder for a client whose Rs 8,500 invoice is 7 days overdue, offering UPI payment, in 3 short lines."
- **Why now:** stricter GST compliance makes clean billing a must-have, not a nice-to-have.
